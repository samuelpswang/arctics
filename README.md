# arctics

**Intro**

Arctics is a Taiwanese video mentoring platform which aims to help secondary school studetns gain firsthand college experiences. Our web app platform will be built on full MERN stack and our landing page is just about to be deployed. 

To find out more about our services, check out our [landing page](https://samuelpswang.com/arctics/) or email us at arctics.academic666@gmail.com.
